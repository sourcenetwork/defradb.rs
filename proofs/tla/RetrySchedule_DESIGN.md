# Sender retry clock

A sender keeps one retry clock per replicator peer (`RetryInfo` under the peer's `ReplicatorRetryIDKey`), shared by every document and collection marker it owes that peer. A pass that lands nothing climbs one rung (`RetryInfo::bump_with_schedule` through `PeerstoreRetryAttempt::Reschedule`); a pass that landed some markers returns to the first rung (`PeerstoreRetryAttempt::Restart`); a connection event makes the clock due at once on the rung it had (`PeerstoreRetryAttempt::Activate`). A new marker leaves an existing clock alone (`RegisterScopeMarkerAttempt`). An acknowledged completion that leaves markers behind restarts the clock, never later than its deadline and never above the first rung (`PeerstoreRetryAttempt::CompleteMarker` calling `RetryInfo::restart_after_delivery`). It covers live acknowledgements recorded by `record_failure` in `crates/p2p-adapter/src/retry.rs` and replay acknowledgements (`complete_document_retry_if_current`) alike, which matters for a pass that `MAX_MARKERS_PER_PEER_PASS` or `MAX_PEER_PASS` cuts short: it fails nothing, so `run_retry_pass` neither charges nor restarts the clock, and only its replays' acknowledgements move it. An acknowledgement whose marker generation a newer write replaced is fenced off the marker and its acknowledged record, and still restarts the clock; `run_push_job` reports it although its job is retired. A completion with no acknowledged heads (a filtered or vanished document) is not a delivery and keeps the clock. The sweep reads the clock through `RetryInfo::bounded_by` (`Peerstore::load_scope_markers`): no rung writes a deadline further ahead than the longest one, so such a deadline, left by a backward step of the wall clock, is due at once.

The model's state is the code's: whether the transport lists the peer, its markers, the clock's rung and deadline, and the push backlog's in-flight jobs and the newer heads queued behind them. It checks one peer through outages, reconnects, writes whose live pushes land, fail or are superseded, passes that stop on a failure or are cut short, backward clock steps, and a document that never lands. GREEN restarts the clock on an acknowledgement, live, superseded or replayed, and on nothing else, and bounds the deadlines it reads. `ACT_DeliveryRestarts` is an action property over the delivery steps; `ACT_SilentPeerBacksOff` says that, while markers are owed, only a delivery lowers the rung and only time, a pass, a reconnect or a delivery brings the deadline closer. `Red_KeepRung` is the rule before this change, `Red_SupersededKeepsRung` restarts only on an acknowledgement whose generation is current, and `Red_ReplayKeepsRung` not on a replayed one; each fails `ACT_DeliveryRestarts`. `Red_RegisterRestarts` restarts on every new marker and `Red_ActivateRestarts` on every reconnect; both fail `ACT_SilentPeerBacksOff`. `Red_ClockBackHolds` reads the stored deadline as it is, and a backward step holds a connected peer past the longest rung (`INV_HeldWithinLongestRung`). The restart REDs take no clock steps, so each fails on its own rule.

Time is abstract ticks, and passes take no time. The bound is on when the peer is next swept; which marker a bounded pass reaches first is the durable dispatch cursor's concern, and receiver backpressure is `RetryPushAdmission.tla`'s. The Lean half, `RetrySchedule.Delivery`, proves the restart's arithmetic over seconds, the receiver deadline and the jittered delay. The Rust regressions are `a_live_ack_returns_an_escalated_peer_to_the_first_rung`, `a_superseded_live_ack_returns_an_escalated_peer_to_the_first_rung`, `a_truncated_pass_that_delivered_returns_the_peer_to_the_first_rung` and `a_failed_live_push_alone_keeps_the_escalated_rung` in `crates/p2p-adapter/src/retry_sweep_tests.rs`, `superseded_acknowledged_push_reports_its_delivery` in `crates/p2p/src/sync/coordinator/push_worker_tests.rs`, and `a_backward_clock_step_cannot_hold_a_ladder_deadline_past_its_longest_rung` in `crates/storage/tests/retry_schedule.rs`.
