# Sender retry clock

A sender keeps one retry clock per replicator peer (`RetryInfo` under the peer's `ReplicatorRetryIDKey`), shared by every document and collection marker it owes that peer. A pass that lands nothing climbs one rung (`RetryInfo::bump_with_schedule` through `PeerstoreRetryAttempt::Reschedule`); a pass that landed some markers returns to the first rung (`PeerstoreRetryAttempt::Restart`); a connection event makes the clock due at once on the rung it had (`PeerstoreRetryAttempt::Activate`). A new marker leaves an existing clock alone (`RegisterScopeMarkerAttempt`). An acknowledged completion that leaves markers behind restarts the clock, never later than its deadline and never above the first rung (`PeerstoreRetryAttempt::CompleteMarker` calling `RetryInfo::restart_after_delivery`). It covers live acknowledgements recorded by `record_failure` in `crates/p2p-adapter/src/retry.rs` and replay acknowledgements (`complete_document_retry_if_current`) alike, which matters for a pass that `MAX_MARKERS_PER_PEER_PASS` or `MAX_PEER_PASS` cuts short: it fails nothing, so `run_retry_pass` neither charges nor restarts the clock, and only its replays' acknowledgements move it; a completion with no acknowledged heads (a filtered or vanished document) is not a delivery and keeps the clock.

The model checks one peer through outages, reconnects, live pushes that land or fail, passes that stop on a failure or are cut short, and a document that never lands. GREEN restarts the clock on an acknowledgement, live or replayed, and on nothing else. `Red_KeepRung` is the rule before this change: an acknowledgement leaves the escalated clock, and `INV_DeliveredPeerDueAtFirstRung` fails. `Red_ReplayKeepsRung` restarts on a live acknowledgement but not on a replayed one: a pass cut short after a replay landed leaves the peer due on the escalated rung, so `INV_DeliveredPeerDueAtFirstRung` holds, but the next timeout charges that rung and `INV_FirstFailureAfterDeliveryChargesSecondRung` fails; the same trace fails it under `Red_KeepRung`. `Red_RegisterRestarts` restarts on every new marker and `Red_ActivateRestarts` on every reconnect; both let a peer that keeps failing be swept before the rung it earned, and `INV_SilentPeerBacksOff` fails.

Time is abstract ticks, and passes take no time. The bound is on when the peer is next swept; which marker a bounded pass reaches first is the durable dispatch cursor's concern, and receiver backpressure is `RetryPushAdmission.tla`'s. The Lean half, `RetrySchedule.Delivery`, proves the restart's arithmetic over seconds, the receiver deadline and the jittered delay. The Rust regressions are `a_live_ack_returns_an_escalated_peer_to_the_first_rung`, `a_truncated_pass_that_delivered_returns_the_peer_to_the_first_rung` and `a_failed_live_push_alone_keeps_the_escalated_rung` in `crates/p2p-adapter/src/retry_sweep_tests.rs`.
